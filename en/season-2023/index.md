## Season 2023

- 2nd – CSIOJ Nations Cup, Šamorín (only double clear)
- 2nd – Schröder Masterclass Grand Prix, Tubbergen
- 3rd – CSIOJ Nations Cup, Zduchovice
- 3rd – CSIOJ 1m35, Praha Velka Chuchle
- 4th – CSIOJ Teams 1m40, Praha Velka Chuchle
- 4th – CSIO4* 1m40, Praha Velka Chuchle
- 5th – CSI1* 1m30 Grand Prix, Lier
- 5th – CSIY 1m35 Grand Prix, Samorin
- 6th – CSIO4* 1m35, Praha Velka Chuchle
- 8th – CSI2* 1m45 Grand Prix, Prague Playoffs
- 10th – CSI2* 1m45, Samorin
- Nominated for the FEI Jumping European Championships for Juniors
